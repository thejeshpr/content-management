## 💡 Investment Tips
- Use SIP mode on platforms like **Groww**, **Zerodha**, or **Dhan** for each stock.  
- Keep the same percentage allocations for the entire 10-year period.  
- Reinvest dividends to maximize compounding returns.  
- Review your portfolio **once a year** and rebalance if required.  
- Stay invested long-term — ignore short-term market volatility.  
- Focus on **consistency, not timing** — SIP discipline builds wealth.
